Welcome to MabiniA coastal municipality in Batangas, Philippines
Get to know the place

Where the land meets a living sea.

Mabini sits on the Calumpang Peninsula in Batangas, between Batangas Bay and Balayan Bay. Its coastal setting, nearby dive sites, and scenic uplands make it a destination for ocean lovers and outdoor explorers.

Its best-known tourism area, Anilao, is especially popular for scuba diving, snorkeling, and underwater photography. Beyond the water, visitors can explore mountain scenery and experience the warmth of local coastal communities.

Know before you go

A smoother trip
starts right here.

A few essentials to help you prepare for your visit, travel responsibly, and support local communities.

Getting there

Travel via Batangas City or nearby transport hubs, then arrange onward transport to Mabini. Confirm current routes and schedules before leaving.

Pack thoughtfully

Bring sun protection, drinking water, suitable footwear, and a dry bag. For diving, use a reputable operator and follow your guide's safety briefing.

Travel responsibly

Never stand on or touch coral. Don't feed marine wildlife. Take your litter with you, and respect community rules and protected areas.

Our story

A coastal town with a story to tell.

Read Mabini's history
1918Mabini was inaugurated as an independent municipality on January 1, 1918, after separating from Bauan.
Its namesakeThe municipality was named after Apolinario Mabini, the Filipino revolutionary thinker often called the “Brains of the Revolution.”
Coastal rootsFishing and coastal livelihoods have long been important to local communities, while tourism has brought wider recognition to Anilao's marine environment.
